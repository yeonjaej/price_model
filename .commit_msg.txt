feat(model): regularized linear cross-sectional baseline (LassoCV)

Diagnostic basis
----------------
The OHLCV LightGBM (extended_kaggle_v2_ohlcv) at refit_freq_days=21
trades ~206x annual turnover and posts a NEGATIVE net-of-cost Sharpe
at 10bp transaction costs. The post-Oct-2022 regime decomposition
showed: (a) the cross-sectional momentum signal lives at 12-24 month
lookbacks, not the 60-day window LightGBM was anchored on; (b)
volatility is the dominant single-feature signal with POSITIVE sign;
(c) the 22-feature monotone-constrained tree does not beat its
1-feature unconstrained variant on either gross IC or net Sharpe -
the model is adding turnover, not signal.

Han, He, Rapach, Zhou (2024, Review of Finance) - "Expected Stock
Returns and the Cross-Section: An E-LASSO Approach" - establish that
Lasso on documented characteristics matches or beats heavier ML on
heavily-arbitraged US large-caps net of trading costs. The mechanism
is automatic feature selection plus slow-changing linear coefficients,
both of which reduce turnover by construction.

What ships
----------
1. LassoCrossSectional model (src/price_model/models/linear.py)
   Pooled LassoCV fit on the (date, ticker) feature panel against y
   (forward excess return). Parameters configurable via config.params:
   alphas, cv (default 5), max_iter (5000), tol (1e-4), selection
   ("cyclic"), fit_intercept (True). Exposes feature_importance()
   (signed coefficients) and selected_alpha() for diagnostics.
   Pickle save/load round-trip. Registered as "LassoCrossSectional"
   in MODEL_REGISTRY.

2. Two new features:

   - momentum_756 (technical.py)
     3-year (756-day) trailing log return. Motivated by the empirical
     finding that ARIMA's annual-refit equivalent window over the
     evaluation period is ~1400 days; 756 days sits between mom_504
     and mom_1260 and is the better fixed-window proxy. The
     cross-sectional Spearman correlation between ARIMA's prediction
     and mom_504 was only +0.43 full-sample, not the +0.85+ a clean
     identification would imply; the gap is the window mismatch.

   - beta_60 / RollingBeta60 (cross_features.py)
     60-day rolling beta of daily log returns to the cross-sectional
     mean log return (equal-weighted universe proxy for market). The
     diagnostic that motivates it: cross-sectional correlation between
     ARIMA's prediction and beta_60 is -0.067 full-sample - the
     project's "beta tilt" alternative explanation for ARIMA's IC is
     empirically falsified. The feature is included primarily as a
     control covariate in the Lasso so coefficients on momentum / vol
     features represent alpha after controlling for market exposure
     (the E-LASSO control structure).

3. Experiment config (config/experiments/lasso_pit.yaml)
   12-feature LassoCV on the PIT-corrected sp500_pit universe:
   - Long-horizon momentum: mom_504, mom_378, mom_12_1, mom_756
   - Vol cluster: vol_20, vol_ewm_20, parkinson_vol_20
   - Size control: log_dollar_volume
   - Beta control: beta_60
   - Tail-event / overbought: max_return_21d, body_ratio, range_ratio
   Annual refit (252d) - Lasso predictions are slow by construction so
   monthly cadence would just add turnover. Z-score normalization per
   date (required for L1 to penalize features uniformly).

4. Tests (tests/test_lasso_model.py, tests/test_cross_features.py,
   tests/test_long_horizon_momentum.py extended)
   - Lasso: fit/predict round-trip, save/load preserves predictions to
     1e-12, feature_importance shape + finiteness, selected_alpha
     positivity, heavy regularization zeros at least one coefficient.
   - beta_60: registration, dtype, 60-row warmup, per-ticker isolation
     under FP tolerance, recovers known beta direction on a 2-ticker
     constructed panel, returns 0 on flat prices.
   - momentum_756: registration, warmup matches lookback (756 nulls),
     math equals log(P_t / P_{t-756}), per-ticker isolation, flat
     prices give zero momentum.

5. conftest.py: synthetic_panel bumped from 600 to 900 trading days so
   the no-leakage truncation-invariance test exercises momentum_756
   (which needs > 756 days of history).

Hypotheses being tested
-----------------------
H_signal: at least one of the long-horizon momentum + volatility
  features survives L1 selection with a non-zero coefficient, and the
  beta_60 / size controls absorb factor exposure without distorting
  the residual signal.

H_turnover: Lasso's slow-changing linear coefficients drive turnover
  below 50x annual (vs ~206x for the 22-feature LightGBM), making net
  Sharpe at 10bp positive for the first time on this project.

Expected wall-clock: ~5-10 min. CLI command to run it:

    price-model run-experiment --config config/experiments/lasso_pit.yaml

After the run, inspect coefficients via model.feature_importance() to
build the model card's "selected features" section.
